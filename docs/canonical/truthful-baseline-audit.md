## 2. Invariants & Regulatory Safeguards

1. **Advisory Decision Support:** AI outputs are advisory recommendations for scrutiny officers. Statutory decisions, physical inspections, and approval/rejection remain solely with AICTE authorities.
2. **Truthful External States:** An unconfigured issuing authority endpoint returns `source_unavailable` or `not_attempted`. Simulated demos are prominently tagged as `simulated_demo`.
3. **No Unverified Rules:** Rules cannot become active without exact APH clause citations, retrieval dates, named reviewers, and four-category test suites (positive, negative, boundary, exception).
4. **Data Minimization:** No personal data or private institutional documents are stored or exposed beyond what is strictly necessary for compliance evaluation.
